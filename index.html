BC Tech Summit 2027

The Cronus Events Business Central conference. 11 and 12 June 2027, Cronus Conference Centre, Amsterdam.

Venue

Cronus Conference Centre, Congresplein 1, Amsterdam. Auditorium A and Auditorium B are on the ground floor, Room 101 and Room 102 on the first floor, and Room 201 and the Workshop Lab on the second floor. The registration desk is in the entrance lobby and opens at 08:00 every day.

Transport and parking

Metro and tram stop Congresplein, 5 minutes on foot; trains from Amsterdam Schiphol airport take about 15 minutes to the nearest station. The venue car park (400 spaces) costs 12 euros per day for attendees; show your badge at the exit. Taxis and ride-hailing pick-up is at the north entrance.

Wi-Fi

Network BCTechSummit, password agents2027. The network is available in all rooms and in the exhibition area.

Catering

Coffee breaks at 10:00 and 14:00 in the exhibition area. Networking lunch from 11:45 to 12:55 in the ground-floor restaurant. Vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options are available every day; tell the catering staff about allergies.

Badges and access

Collect your badge at the registration desk with the QR code from your confirmation email. The badge gives access to all sessions, the exhibition area and the networking dinner on 11 June (Terrace, 20:00). Speakers collect their badge at the speaker desk next to Auditorium A.

Code of conduct

BC Tech Summit is a harassment-free event. Report any issue to a member of staff (orange lanyard) or write to conduct@cronusevents.example.
